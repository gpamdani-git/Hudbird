# Hudbird Homepage Direction

This file applies only to the documentation homepage at `/`. It does not replace the global
`DESIGN.md` rules used by the wider component catalogue.

## Design read

- ENERGY 1: quiet, product-led, and low decoration.
- RHYTHM 2: open editorial spacing with a clear reading order.
- MOTION 2: one-time short reveals and brief crossfades for user-controlled changes.
- Reference: Linear's layout clarity, typographic hierarchy, and product proof. Do not copy its
  identity or page structure.

## Homepage overrides

- Use Outfit for display headings and Plus Jakarta Sans for body and controls.
- Prefer open typographic rows to bento mosaics and repeated feature cards.
- Do not add uppercase eyebrow labels above primary headings.
- Do not add decorative motion loops, scroll hijacking, ambient glow fields, or auto-advancing
  product controls.
- Show the Hudbird product or rendered Hudbird components instead of generic illustration.
- Build every marketing section from Hudbird `Section`, `Container`, and `Grid` through the public
  marketing blocks.
- Keep claims verifiable. Customer logos, usage statistics, testimonials, and performance claims
  require an approved source before publication.
